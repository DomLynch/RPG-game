FIELD JOURNAL
Move with the left stick. Push to its outer edge to sprint.
Light → light → heavy for a combo (the heavy comes out faster). A light straight out of a step or roll is quick too. After a parry: light thrusts (24), heavy smashes (30). Heavy breaks guard.
Hold guard to block (raise it just in time and it costs half); tap just before impact to parry. Tap guard early in your own swing to feint it. Attacks come straight out of a guard.
Tap dodge to step back (no invulnerability); hold it to roll. Kick at close range to open a guard (25 stamina).
Sword wounds reduce stamina recovery 20% for 4 seconds. Rematch clears them.
