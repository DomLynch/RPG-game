# Playtest 1 — five people, five phones, no coaching

Strategy, 2026-09-24. Runs on the first sha where the parry fix and charge pose B are both live (gate: the defence-audit sheet rules yes). Dom supplies the five people. Nobody from the team plays; nobody explains anything.

## Setup (2 min per person)

- Their own phone, Safari or Chrome, https://frankendom.com, guest. Sound ON, no headphones needed.
- Say only: "It's a sword duel. Guard, attack, kick, step. Try to win." Nothing else, ever. If they ask how something works, say "try it and tell me what you think happened."
- One observer per player, or one observer rotating. Observer holds a phone recording the player's screen if possible; otherwise notes only.

## Run (15 min per person)

Three fights against the default opponent, then one against any other they pick. Between fights, nothing is said.

## The three questions

Ask after every exchange where the player took damage, was guard-broken, or blocked/parried/dodged. Ask exactly these, write the answer verbatim, do not correct it:

1. **What just happened?**
2. **Why did you take damage?** (or, when they didn't: **What did that defence get you?**)
3. **What would you try next?**

Also note, per exchange, without asking: did they see the charge coming (a look, a hand move, a word), and did they tell the parry from the block without the text. When their posture breaks, note whether they showed any sign of knowing it before the critical landed (the bot cannot measure this; the playtest is the only instrument).

One observer runs a listen check before the session starts, phone sound on: when the opponent winds up a heavy, a rising cue should climb for the whole hold and stop dead on a feint or a stagger. Note yes/no once; it is not a player question.

## Scoring sheet (one row per exchange)

| player | fight | exchange | what they said happened | why they said | next they'd try | saw the charge? | parry ≠ block? | observer: what actually happened |

The last column is filled from the recording or the observer's own eyes, after the session, never in front of the player.

## What we are measuring (not shown to players)

- **Charge readability:** share of charged heavies the player named before impact. Baseline = the bot's limited-observation numbers.
- **Defence feedback:** share of blocks/parries/dodges the player named correctly in Q1, and whether Q2 names a cause the game actually showed.
- **Guard-broken line:** whether Q2 after a guard break repeats the cause line or invents one.
- **Next-action quality:** whether Q3 is a real option in the game (roll, parry, kick, wait) or "hit it harder."

## After

Observers hand the sheets to Lead the same evening; Lead sends me one table (rows above, all five). I rule on what moves in the presentation pass and what stays parked. No mechanic changes come out of this run; timings, damage and Easy are frozen for beta.
