## Strategy notes on the picks
- Two picks rely on a screen-space distortion (Knight Wrath, and Pitborn Heat Shimmer if chosen): build a cheap version first and check phone cost.
- Goblin Three Liars and Witch Foretold Step must keep the real fighter obvious; afterimages under ~0.4 s.
- Witch The Price drains colour, the Centurion's Blood Tithe tints red: different enough, but never both in one fight.
