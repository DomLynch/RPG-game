# Centurion boss specials, ranks 8, 9, 10 — brief (Strategy, 2026-10-01)

Dom picked all three on 2026-10-01: "yes all to A your pick, get it done give me the clip examples".
The Centurion's legends at these ranks (src/legends.ts, `veteran`): L8 Ajax, L9 Alexander, L10 Mars.
At these ranks he carries the gladius and the tower shield.

## The three moves

| Rank | Legend | Move | Reads from | Lane |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Ajax | **Shield Quake** | below | Veteran (the Centurion's lane) |
| 9 | Alexander | **The Charge** | across the ground | World, with Audio for the hooves |
| 10 | Mars | **Blood Tithe** | the whole arena | Finishers & Gore |

1. **Shield Quake (Ajax).** He lifts the tower shield and drives its rim into the sand. A ripple of sand runs
   along the ground from the shield to the opponent and bursts up under them. The burst is the hit.
2. **The Charge (Alexander).** No horse is shown. The sound of hooves builds, and a line of dust and trembling
   sand races across the arena toward the opponent. He arrives out of the dust with the blow.
3. **Blood Tithe (Mars).** The arena light turns red and the crowd's roar swells. Red dust lifts from the sand
   all over the arena and pours into his blade, then one strike.

## Rules (same as the Nightborn set; do not relitigate)

- Unblockable and undodgeable once released; ~2 s wind-up in the sim; 30 % damage; 20 s cooldown. The sim
  wiring is Combat's (#1114) after the Saturday 3 Oct duel. These clips are presentation-only previews.
- Each move is one clean idea. No added props: no horse, no spears, no hammer, no floating objects.
- Dom's taste, learned on the Nightborn set:
  - Grounded and real, made from the arena's own sand, dust and light. Painted and irregular, never
    evenly drawn or symmetrical ("like the blood", not "a low-quality MMO").
  - No glow, no neon, no orange fire, no plastic-looking cylinders or perfect stars.
  - The visible build-up is fast: ~0.4–0.6 s to the payoff. A slow 1–2 s build-up was rejected on Red Wind.
  - Semi-transparent where it is air or dust; it must not hide both fighters for long.
- Mars may be a touch more than human (he is a god); Ajax and Alexander stay physical.
