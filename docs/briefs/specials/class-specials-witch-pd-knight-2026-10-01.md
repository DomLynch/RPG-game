# Class specials: Witch, Plague Doctor, Knight — proposals for Dom's pick (Weapons, 2026-10-01)

Asked by Lead 2026-10-01, with Nightborn's picked set (Pale Lunge + Seven Cuts) as the model. Nothing is built; Dom picks, then Weapons builds
presentation-only previews. Boss specials (ranks 8–10) are a separate brief: boss-specials-proposals-2026-10-01.md.

## Rules (Dom's, from Lead)

- Two slots per character: **A** for ranks 1–3, **B** for ranks 4–7. Three takes each; ★ is Weapons' pick.
- 20 % of max health, every 20 s, unblockable, ~2 s tell, grounded, smaller than the boss specials.
- Nothing pale or glowing over the fighters. Every line, streak or mark is **darker than the floor** (wet sand, ground shadow, scuffed
  earth), made from the arena's own sand and dust. No props added; each take uses the weapon the character already carries.
- Painted and irregular, never even or symmetrical. Presentation only: the sim wiring is Combat's.

Weapons carried: Witch = the mage staff (trident-family carry), Plague Doctor = the estoc, Knight = the maul.

## Witch (staff)

**A, ranks 1–3**
1. ★ **Stone Wake** — she drags the staff foot through the sand toward the foe; a dark, damp furrow opens along the ground and runs at them,
   the blow lands where it ends.
2. **Root Rise** — she plants the staff; two or three dark cracks fan out, sand lifts in a hand-width ridge under the foe, then the strike.
3. **Hush** — she stands still, grit settles flat round her feet, the crowd noise dips for a beat, then one short stab.

**B, ranks 4–7**
1. ★ **Stirring** — she circles the staff head low over the sand; a dark ring of stirred sand widens round the foe's feet and tightens,
   the staff comes up and lands.
2. **Heavy Step** — a single slow stamp, a dark ring of settled dust shrugs out from her heel, strike on the ring's return.
3. **Drawn Line** — she scratches a dark line across the sand with the staff foot, steps over it, and the foe is hit as they cross it.

## Plague Doctor (estoc)

**A, ranks 1–3**
1. ★ **Doctor's Tempo** — a slow, deliberate walk-in, three measured steps leaving three dark prints in the sand, the estoc held out
   level; the thrust lands on the third beat.
2. **Sleeve Draw** — the estoc is drawn from his coat sleeve with a long dark scrape on the sand, then the thrust. Only if the model shows a sheath or sleeve to draw from.
3. **Bow and Point** — a short formal bow, the beak dips, the point comes up and he lunges from the bow.

**B, ranks 4–7**
1. ★ **Taking the Pulse** — he reaches the left hand toward the foe's wrist, dust on the ground tightens in a dark ring round the foe, and
   the estoc follows on the beat.
2. **Beak Down** — the beak lowers, dark dust rises from the sand beside the foe like breath, one clean thrust.
3. **Rot Mark** — the point scratches a dark blotch into the sand at the foe's feet, which spreads a hand-width; the thrust lands inside it.

## Knight (maul)

**A, ranks 1–3**
1. ★ **Ground Drag** — the maul head drags a dark, rutted trench through the sand toward the foe, clods lifting, and swings up into the blow
   at the trench's end.
2. **Shoulder Lean** — he drops the shoulder and the maul behind him, dust slides off his armour, a heavy two-step and one overhead blow.
3. **Plant** — the maul is set down and he leans on it; the sand dishes in a dark ring under the head, then he heaves it up and over.

**B, ranks 4–7**
1. ★ **Held Swing** — he winds the maul back and holds, the arena stills and dust hangs, the sand shivers in a dark ring round his feet, then
   one wide flat swing.
2. **Ring the Anvil** — a single maul strike on the sand, a dark ring of kicked grit runs out, the follow-up lands on the foe.
3. **Wall Step** — he squares up, takes a heavy step in, dark scuffs of sand fan from the boots, and a short straight blow.
