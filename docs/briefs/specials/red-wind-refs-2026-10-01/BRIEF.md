# Red Wind (Set, Nightborn L8) — three new directions after v6 (Dom, 2026-10-01 08:5x)

Dom on v6: "better colour, but still looks like a manufacturing-plant circular piece of plastic", i.e. a clean
even cylinder of thin threads. The five reference images in this folder are what "wind" means to him:
BROAD soft ribbons and sheets with painted, torn, motion-blurred edges, varied width, overlapping and
semi-transparent, with grit and debris carried along. Never thin uniform lines, never a tidy cylinder.

Common build rule for all three: painted ribbon/sheet sprites (GPT paints them, see the eight-jobs brief
Job 2; NO ZeroGPU/FLUX on our HF account), 6–10 distinct sprites per option, each a different length, width,
curl and opacity, scrolled and rotated by the lane; grey-white with a darker grey core, semi-transparent;
sand grit and a few pebbles lifted off the arena floor; fighter stays readable. One clean idea per option.

## Option A — Ground burst (ref 1)
Set stamps; a ring of sand erupts outward at the target's feet in 8–12 radial streaks of different lengths,
with 3–4 broad curved sheets peeling up and over the target like a wave breaking, and gravel arcing out.
Peak 0.4 s, fades as the sheets dissolve. Reads as "the arena floor itself hit you".

## Option B — Spiral updraft (refs 3, 5)
Three or four wide painted ribbons wind upward around the target in a loose, uneven spiral (brush-stroke
widths: one fat band, two medium, one thin), each on a different radius and lean, tops tearing into wisps
above the head. Not a cylinder: the ribbons do not close, gaps are visible, the spiral tilts. Ring of
lifted sand at the base. 1.2 s build, 0.6 s tear-away.

## Option C — Wind wall (refs 2, 4)
A wall of air sweeps THROUGH the target from Set's side: two overlapping vertical soft sheets, 1.5 m tall,
torn upper edge, motion-blurred, crossing the target in 0.3 s and leaving a drift of sand and two or three
trailing wisps behind. The quietest and most "atmosphere" of the three; the hit is the sheet passing.

Deliverable per option: light-arena clip + peak still at the fight camera, 375 wide, side by side as one
contact sheet, so Dom picks one in one look. No code-drawn lines; painted sprites only.
