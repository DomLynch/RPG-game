**How to read this.** Each rank is a different named legend, so "a different person from its neighbours" is by design and is NOT flagged on its own. Method: a look at each sheet at 300 px per portrait, no measurements. Treat each line as a candidate for GPT to repaint, not a verdict. Characters with no flag beyond these lines are consistent.
