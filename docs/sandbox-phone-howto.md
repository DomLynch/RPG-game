# Sandbox phone test: two specials, one fight

1. Open https://frankendom.com/preview/sandbox/?spar=1&weapon=longsword&difficulty=dummy&skill=pommel&opponent=Centurion on your phone and tap Fight. SPECIAL 1 is Pommel Strike and SPECIAL 2 is Butcher's Cleave (placeholder default).
2. To pick others, tap Sandbox, scroll to Loadout, choose "Your special 1" and "Your special 2" (lunge, ironrush, pommel, shove, cleave, stomp; slot 2 also has Heal), then Apply loadout + reload and tap Fight. Or add `&skill=<id>&special2=<id>` to the link.
3. Wait about 2 seconds after Fight, tap SPECIAL 1 or SPECIAL 2 next to the Dummy (it never attacks), and watch the flash at the hit; tell us which impact is too small or too big.
