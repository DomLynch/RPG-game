# Origins launch gates

(Strategy, 2026-10-07.) This file is the one home for every "before X opens" gate in Origins.

A **gate** is a condition that must hold before a surface opens to players. Nothing in a section opens until every gate in that section
is closed with its proof. A gate closes only when the proof is recorded in its row (a receipt: a sha, a command's output, a PR, or a
ruling with its date). Add new gates here, not in other docs; other docs link here.

Each row: ID | gate | owner (the lane that closes it) | proof that closes it | status (open or closed, with the receipt).

**Reading the readout (Backend's review of #3145):**
- The diagnostics code and the `strictFlip` row take effect only after a writer restart: a release, or a restart by Deploy.
- The reach and record counters live in the writer's memory, and every writer restart or deploy sets them back to zero. `ready` therefore counts only the kills since the last restart: read it after the soak has run and **before** the flag-flip restart.
- Flipping a flag is an env change plus a restart, and that restart zeroes the counters again. So the receipt is the readout taken just before the flip, kept with its `since`.
- The two counters have different denominators. The reach tally counts every kill report that got past the token check, including kills that are then refused or left unverified for their record (`sheet` and the like). The record tally counts kills by their record outcome (`no record`, `verified` or a refusal reason), and with the flag off a verdict lands only after the replay finishes. So the two totals need not match at any one moment.
