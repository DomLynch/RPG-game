# Spell look stills (2026-10-17): Heal and Ash Fall, before and after

Captured in the in-app browser (real GPU) from the live preview: `canvas.toDataURL` in page JS on the fight camera, a dummy fight, `?spell=<id>&cast=100`, the player casting. Each AFTER file is three frames of one cast (6, 24 and 50 ticks after the landing: about 0.1 s, 0.4 s and 0.8 s), which is the clip substitute: there is no video encoder in this environment, so the "2-second clip per spell" Strategy asked for is NOT produced; a screen recording on the phone is the way to get it.

| Spell | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Heal | `heal-BEFORE-v1-3-frames.jpg`: a flat translucent green tube and ring | `heal-AFTER-v2-3-frames.jpg`: a rim-lit column of light with grain that erodes, a ground ring, climbing glints, a short light |
| Ash Fall | `ash-fall-BEFORE-designed-effect.jpg`: the designed special effect's flat red-brown dust sheet (one frame, the peak) | `ash-fall-AFTER-3-frames.jpg`: an orange flash and ground ring, embers thrown up, a dark ash plume that rises and erodes |

Not captured: the dust shock, Wind Wall and Nyx stills (they were written in the same pass but not inspected), and the frame-time measurement on the phone.
