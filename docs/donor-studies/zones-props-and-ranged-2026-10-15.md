# Donor study: zone props as data, and ranged weapons (zone sandbox, 2026-10-15)

Asked by Dom. Method: the donor library (`/mnt/frankendom-donors/_donor-library`, deep notes were thin for these cells, so a Sonnet agent read raw source on the VPS). Licence rule: MIT donors (World of Claudecraft = `levy-street-world-of-claudecraft`) may be studied closely; GPL/AGPL/MPL donors (Veloren, openmw, AzerothCore, EQEmu, ModernUO, OpenDAoC, forgottenserver, Flare, Cubyz) are learn-the-mechanism, rewrite from the idea. Not verified: EQEmu spawn2/grid tables, openmw cell/statics format.

## A. Zones and props as data (this lane)
Best practice:
- **Zone record** (WoC `src/sim/types.ts:4222` ZoneDef): id, bounds, level range, a biome id (17-value union) that keys palette, sky, music and motes tables (`render/terrain_palette.ts:14`).
- **Camp** (WoC `types.ts:3998` CampDef `{mobId, center, radius, count, offStream}`): deterministic sunflower spiral with jitter scaled to spacing (`sim/camp_scatter.ts`); `offStream` = the camp's own seeded RNG, so adding a camp never reshuffles the world.
- **Props per zone** (WoC `ZonePropsDef`, `types.ts:4359`): typed arrays per kind (tents, fences, `ruinRings {x,z,ringR,columns}`, `decorProps {key,x,z,rot,r,h,scale}`); weak spot: hand-typed ring coordinates. Flora (`render/realm_flora.ts:1-25`) from a deterministic hash grid of the world seed, skipping roads, plateaus, water.
- **Scatter table** (Veloren `world/src/layer/scatter.rs:48-120`, GPL): `{kind, water_mode, permit(surface), density(chunk,column), clump noise}`; density = closeness to ideal climate x tree_density x factor; clump noise makes patches. **Spots** (`common/src/spot.rs:60-84`): composable `All/Any/Not` condition tree.
- **Biome record** (Cubyz, GPL): one record = climate flags, vegetation, height range, fog/sky, `chance` weight.
- **Statics and spawners as plain data** (ModernUO, GPL): statics text files; spawner JSON `{location, count, delay, homeRange, entries[{name, maxCount, probability}]}`.
Take for Frankendom (proposed tables; Strategy ruled: generic `origins/world/props-defs.json`, goblin-camp is the first kit, dress-density first):
- `prop`: key, kit, glb (or parts), r (collider), h, scaleMin/Max, yawMode, tint, clearance, walkThrough.
- `kit`: id, biome, entries[{propKey, weight}], rarityWeight, levelBand.
- `recipe`: id, kind (scatter | cluster | ring | line | spiral), kitId, count or density, radiusMin/Max, minSpacing, jitter, arc, seedSalt (own RNG sub-stream), avoid[water|road|slope|pad].
- `dress-density`: zone or biome, layer (tree | rock | flower | camp), basePerAcre, clump wavelength/threshold, exclusions, seedSalt.
- Rules: every placement is a pure function of (zone seed, row id); each recipe row has its own RNG; conditions composable; collision rows separate from art rows.

## B. Ranged weapons and spells (Combat sandbox's lane; handed over, not built here)
Best practice:
- **Missile**: travelling object, hit rolled at landing, fizzle if either end is gone (WoC `projectile_travel.ts`: homing 26 yd/s, reach 0.7, 3 s max). AzerothCore rolls at launch and delays the effect by distance (`Spell.cpp:2474-2486`); openmw arrows get weak gravity (0.1x, `projectilemanager.cpp:512-515`), magic bolts straight; tunnelling guard = a ray/sphere of one step each fixed tick (Daggerfall `DaggerfallMissile.cs:333-352`; Veloren segment test `projectile.rs:660-725`).
- **Draw / charge / reload / ammo**: draw strength scales speed and damage and one ammo is spent on release (openmw `weaponanimation.cpp:133-168`); Veloren charged shot = buildup, charge (energy drain), release, recover; OpenDAoC ranged states None/Aim/ReadyToFire/Fire/AimFire/AimFireReload; UO bows need 0.25-1 s standing still, misses bank ammo, 40% refund on a hit; forgottenserver ammo `breakChance`.
- **Spell table columns**: EQEmu `spdat.h:1604-1680` (range, aoe, cast/recovery/recast, mana, 12 effect slots, resist, target, uninterruptable, npc_no_los, reflectable); WoC AbilityDef (`types.ts:3727-3766`: cost, castTime, channel, castWhileMoving, uninterruptible, cooldown, range, minRange, school, projectile, projectileFx, castFx); Flare power type fixed|missile|repeater|spawn.
- **Visuals**: per-school colour with HDR core x2.5 and trail x1.4, school picks the sprite family (WoC `render/vfx.ts:58-92`); element picks billboards, a light on the missile, a separate impact animation (Daggerfall); one launch sound and a distinct impact sound; ammo-specific shoot effects (forgottenserver `const.h:182-233`).
- **Defence**: line of sight for ranged and non-physical schools (WoC `ability_line_of_sight.ts`); distance-banded hit chance with a miss landing on a random adjacent tile (forgottenserver `weapons.cpp:674-771`); some projectiles unblockable; flat archery hit penalty (EQEmu).
Take for Frankendom (proposed): `ranged` {id, kind bow|crossbow|staff|wand, drawMs, holdMaxMs, reloadMs, speedMin/Max, dmgMin/Max, minStillMs, ammoType, arc}; `ammo` {id, weaponKind, dmgMod, breakPct, recoverPct, tint, flightFx, impactFx}; `spell` {id, school, manaCost, castMs, recoverMs, cooldownMs, interruptOnHit, range, minRange, losRequired, effectId, projectileFx, castFx, impactFx, soundCue, tellColor}; gravityK (0 for bolts); at 375 wide: a draw bar with a loud colour change at "ready", a big core + short trail per school, launch and impact sounds, lane dodge/block removes the bolt, a hit while drawing/casting interrupts with a visible fizzle. Combat sandbox already has: mana pool, one missile model in the outer band, three lanes, finite quiver; it is missing the visual language, the draw/reload beats, arcs, ammo types and a spell table.

## Cross-check against the full audit (DomLynch/Games-Analysis @5413bf2, 2026-10-12; copy at ~/Developer/Games-Analysis)
My first pass used only the 124-game storage-box matrix (thin notes for these cells) plus raw source. The full audit (161 games' notes, `deep/<category>/`, `docs/TOP-70-LEARNINGS-2026-10-12.md`, `docs/BUILD-LIST-FROM-AUDIT-2026-10-12.md`) agrees and adds, relevant to this lane and to Combat sandbox:
- **TOP-70 #2: "a new boss, weapon, finisher, spell or zone is one data row, never a code change."** This is the bar the zone-91 proof is measured against.
- **Build list B54 "Asset sets" already names our gaps:** biome ground textures, a prop kit, weapon trails (large, L3 pending Dom, TRELLIS.2). The goblin camp parts kit and the ground recipe are the cheap first version of the first two; weapon trails belong to the ranged work.
- **Spell rules (TOP-70 #18-#33), for Combat:** magic rides the special-move rows (a spell names a template and its numbers); a spell row holds its own cost and recovery (charges or a cooldown, never a second bar: note our sandbox added a mana pool, check with Strategy); a school (fire, frost, shadow) with per-creature resist numbers in its own row; only landed damage cuts a cast, the earlier the longer the lockout, no build casts faster than half base time; skills and spells are rows naming the rank they unlock at.
- The audit's stated limit stands: most of the ~2,260 partial/none cells were not read in depth (a spot check found about a third hide a usable mechanism), and 82 folders (mostly clients and tools; real servers among them: mangoszero, vmangos, ProjectWAR, PSForever, Holocore) have no notes of their own. I read none of the wave-2 catalogue files for projectiles or vegetation beyond a name search, so section A/B above is a floor, not the whole answer.
