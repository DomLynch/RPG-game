Frankendom Origins — privacy

Updated 19 September 2026

Frankendom Origins is a browser sword-fighting game operated by Dom Lynch. You can practise without creating an account.

What we store

Guest fighter details, settings and practice statistics are stored in your browser. Clearing this site's browser data removes those local records.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your basic account identity, including your email address, name and profile image, with our authentication service, Supabase. We use this identity to sign you in and associate your saves with your account. We do not request access to your Gmail mailbox, contacts or files.

The optional cloud save currently contains only your fighter name, selected practice opponent and a save revision. Saving and loading are explicit actions in the Field Journal. Practice scores and career progress are not uploaded by this feature. Supabase stores your account and cloud save; a session in your browser keeps you signed in.

Service providers and diagnostics

Google provides sign-in, Supabase provides authentication and storage, and Sentry receives technical error reports so we can fix game failures. Our error reporting removes user details, request information and interaction breadcrumbs; it does not record gameplay video. Hosting and service providers may process connection metadata such as IP addresses and service logs when serving the game. We do not sell your Google account data or use it for advertising.

Your choices and deletion

Sign out in the Field Journal on shared devices. Signing out removes the local account session but keeps the device's ordinary fighter settings. You can clear those settings using your browser's site-data controls. You can also revoke Frankendom's access in your Google Account settings; this does not itself delete your cloud save.

We retain your account and cloud save until you request deletion or the service is retired. To request access, correction or deletion of your account and save, contact domlynchdubai@gmail.com from the email linked to your account. Provider security logs and backups follow those providers' retention processes.
