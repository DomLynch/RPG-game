The arena

At the edge of every world

Dead civilisations bleed into one ruined arena. Legions, smiths, gods and fen-monsters all end up on the same sand.

You walk in as a Recruit, in rags, with a longsword. Nobody knows your name.

The hundred

Ten foes. Ten names each.

Every opponent wears a legend at each rank, taken from myth, folklore, ancient history or a book older than 1900. You do not beat the Pitborn at Champion. You beat Grendel.
